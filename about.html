About CinemaDaily

CinemaDaily is an independent digital publication providing English-language coverage of Iranian cinema, film, television, streaming and entertainment.

Our coverage focuses on Iranian films, filmmakers, actors, television series, streaming productions, film festivals, awards, box office developments and other major stories shaping Iran's entertainment industry.

CinemaDaily also follows the international presence of Iranian cinema, including festival selections, awards, international releases and the work of Iranian filmmakers and performers around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make news and information about Iranian cinema more accessible to international audiences through timely, clear and reliable English-language reporting.

CinemaDaily publishes breaking news, interviews, industry updates, reviews, box office reports, trailers and original features while maintaining a direct connection with audiences through its website and social media platforms.

Editorial Coverage

CinemaDaily covers both established and emerging figures in Iranian cinema and television, as well as major productions, festivals, streaming platforms and industry developments.

Our goal is to build a searchable English-language archive of Iranian cinema news and create a reliable reference point for readers, journalists, researchers and cinema audiences around the world.
