Rambod Javan has entered the pre-production stage of his latest entertainment project, a competition show tentatively titled *FestivAI*, centered on creating works of art using AI.

The program aims to offer a new experience combining art, entertainment, and AI. *FestivAI* is currently the working title of the project. The streaming platform that will release the show, as well as the date for the public call for participants, will be announced soon.

*Carnival*, produced and released last year, was Rambod Javan’s most recent project as a television and entertainment show creator.
