After opening to a record-setting $355 million domestically and heroic $932 million globally, “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” has now generated a staggering $655 million in North America and $1.67 billion globally. Sony and Marvel’s web-slinging adventure, starring Tom Holland as the title hero, flew past the billion-dollar mark in just six days and is barreling toward $2 billion. At this pace, box office watchers are wondering whether “Spidey” has the staying power to become the first movie to ever reach $1 billion at the domestic box office and become the sole film to hit $3 billion globally. “The Force Awakens” is currently the highest-grossing release in domestic box office history with $936.6 million, while 2009’s “Avatar” and “Avengers: Endgame” stand as the biggest movies worldwide with $2.9 billion and $2.7 billion, respectively, after several re-releases.



In a distant second place on domestic box office charts, “[The Odyssey](https://variety.com/t/the-odyssey/)” remained muscular with $31.5 million in its fourth weekend of release — a 38% decline. With $461 million domestically and $1.1 billion worldwide, “The Odyssey” officially ranks as Christopher Nolan’s highest-grossing movie of all time, overtaking 2012’s “The Dark Knight Rises” ($1.08 billion) and 2009’s “The Dark Knight” ($1.008 billion) to notch the achievement.
