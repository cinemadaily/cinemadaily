Fifty-five years after Behrouz Vossoughi’s iconic portrayal of Dash Akol, Amir Jadidi is set to take on the legendary character in a new adaptation. According to Cinemadaily, the project will be directed by acclaimed Iranian filmmaker Hamid Nematollah and produced as a series for Iran’s home entertainment network.

The new “Dash Akol” series, based on Sadegh Hedayat’s celebrated story, is being developed for the **Upera** streaming platform, with Jadidi playing the title role. The character was famously portrayed by Vossoughi in Masoud Kimiai’s classic 1971 film, making Jadidi’s casting a notable new chapter for one of Iranian cinema’s most memorable characters.
