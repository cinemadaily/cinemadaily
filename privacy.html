Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

CinemaDaily respects the privacy of its visitors. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you visit cinemadaily.ca, how that information may be used and the role of third-party services.

Information We May Collect

When you visit CinemaDaily, limited technical and usage information may be collected automatically. This may include:

  • Browser and device information

  • Referring pages or websites

If you contact CinemaDaily directly, we may also receive the information you voluntarily provide in your message, such as your name, email address and the content of your communication.

How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate, maintain and improve the website, understand how visitors interact with CinemaDaily, respond to communications and support website security and performance.

Analytics

CinemaDaily may use website analytics services to understand visitor activity, page performance, traffic sources and general usage patterns.

These services may collect information through cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

Cookies

CinemaDaily may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, analytics, performance measurement and improving the visitor experience.

Third-Party Services

CinemaDaily contains links to third-party platforms and services, including Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp and YouTube.

When you visit or interact with a third-party service, its own privacy policy and terms apply. CinemaDaily does not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

Advertising

CinemaDaily may display advertising or work with advertising and commercial partners.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalize advertising according to their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

External Links

Articles and other pages on CinemaDaily may contain links to external websites. CinemaDaily is not responsible for the content, security or privacy practices of websites operated by third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

CinemaDaily may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or operating practices.

When this policy is updated, the revision date shown on this page may also be updated.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be sent to info@cinemadaily.ca or submitted through our Contact page .
