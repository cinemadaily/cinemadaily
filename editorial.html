Editorial Policy

CinemaDaily is committed to providing accurate, timely and clear English-language coverage of Iranian cinema, film, television, streaming and entertainment.

Editorial Independence

Editorial decisions at CinemaDaily are made independently. News coverage, headlines and editorial judgments are based on news value, relevance and the interests of our readers.

Accuracy

We aim to verify information before publication and clearly distinguish confirmed information from reports, claims, speculation and commentary.

Names of filmmakers, actors, films, television series, festivals and other entities are presented using consistent English-language spellings whenever possible.

Sources

CinemaDaily may rely on original reporting, interviews, official announcements, production companies, filmmakers, actors, distributors, streaming platforms, film festivals, public statements and other credible sources.

When CinemaDaily independently reports information, the article may identify the information as being reported by CinemaDaily. When information originates from another publication or source, attribution should be provided when appropriate.

Corrections

If a factual error is identified, CinemaDaily may update or correct the article as soon as reasonably possible.

Readers and sources can report possible errors or request corrections by contacting: info@cinemadaily.ca .

Updates

Developing stories may be updated as new information becomes available. Significant new information may be incorporated into an existing article or published as a new report.

Reviews and Opinion

Reviews, analysis and opinion pieces reflect the judgment of their respective authors or CinemaDaily's editorial team and are distinct from factual news reporting.

Advertising and Partnerships

Advertising, commercial partnerships and sponsored content do not determine CinemaDaily's independent editorial coverage. Sponsored or promotional material should be identified when applicable.

Contact

For editorial questions, corrections, news tips or other inquiries, contact CinemaDaily at info@cinemadaily.ca .
